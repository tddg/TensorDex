2 · Runtime and progress

Wall time 21,228 s (5 h 54 m). The byte curve is front-loaded (largest base-groups scheduled first); the pair curve accelerates in the small-tensor tail where the run becomes request-rate-bound rather than bandwidth-bound.

3 · Where the time went (per-pair stage timing, summed)

S3 I/O (fetch + upload) is 87% of aggregate stage time; TensorX compute is 5%. Compression at hub scale is I/O-bound — the paper’s claim, observed in the wild.

5 · Validation and fidelity

  • Per-pair integrity (100% coverage): every one of the 581,274 deltas was decompressed against its base and XXH3-128-verified byte-exact before upload. 0 mismatches.
  • Against the published artifact (TensorX): on the 195,839 pairs (12.79 TB) where the paper’s results.db holds a measured tratio for the identical (target, base): our weighted tratio 0.3063 vs published 0.3068; per-pair relative difference 0.0000% at p50 and p95; 98.2% byte-identical (the 1.77% that differ are sub-MB tensors, 91% smaller on our newer zstd).
  • Paper claims: TensorDex-TX 65.1% reproduced exactly offline from the AE cache; realized 60.9% here, the 4.2-point gap fully attributed to corpus decay (5.31 TB of registered tensors — including 1,058 of the paper’s 7,948 bases — have no blobs in S3).
  • FM++ (measurement-only; hub stays TensorX): 500-pair stratified sample — 500/500 roundtrips byte-exact, 400/400 published fratios reproduced byte-identically; sample-informed projection validates the 70.5% claim at 71.1% on the paper corpus (66.8% on the surviving corpus).
  • Whole-model reconstruction: 8/8 random servable models rebuilt byte-exact end-to-end from the hybrid layout (deltas from compressed_full/ + bases from tensordb/), every tensor hash-verified; cold single-client read amplification 1.07–1.55. Of 2,892 ready models, 2,530 (87.5%) are fully servable; 362 lost ≥1 tensor to GC.

6 · Process

  1. Census & corpus truth. Registry: 742,027 tensors / 40.1 TB / 2,894 models. Full-prefix LIST: 99,063 registered tensors (5.31 TB, 13.4%) have no blob — silent metadata/blob divergence at hub scale. Physical corpus: 642,964 tensors / 34.81 TB.
  2. Fingerprint recovery — no 40 TB sketch pass. Live master.db fps are an old 256-dim format; the paper-config BCS sketches (d=2, w=1024) were recovered from backups/node_0..15 DBs, bit-exact vs today’s kernel; 167 stragglers sketched fresh. 100% coverage of the physical corpus.
  3. Planning. A NumPy/BLAS reimplementation of the Rust incremental planner (verified bit-equivalent) plans 643k tensors in 25 s — but predicts weighted CR only 0.534. Diffing against the paper’s shipped plan exposed the gap (11% same-base choices; theirs realizes 0.333), so the campaign adopts the paper’s own plan: 552,781 surviving pairs + 28,718 fallback + AE conflict resolution, depth-1 everywhere.
  4. Execution. 28 processes × base-grouped 4 GB units: fetch → TensorX L1 → roundtrip verify → wrap (engine blob format) → upload. Resumable per-pair JSONL ledger; 245 cross-dtype pairs skipped raw.
  5. Catalog. compressed_full/master.db: full schema + tensor_deltas (581,254 edges), per-tensor storage_uri/physical/logical bytes, missing:// flags for GC’d tensors.
